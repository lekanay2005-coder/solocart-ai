Security center

Understand how this local prototype protects your account.

✓

Password protection

Passwords are hashed with scrypt and a unique random salt before they are stored.

◉

Private session

Sign-in uses an HttpOnly, SameSite cookie. The session expires automatically and is cleared when you log out.

⌁

Request safeguards

Same-origin checks, basic login throttling, and security headers protect account requests.

!

Prototype scope

Accounts are stored locally on this server. No email verification, recovery, passkeys, or multi-server session store is configured.
